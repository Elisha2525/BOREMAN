INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS
- IQR flags are review indicators, not proof of erroneous measurements. No values were deleted.
- Correlations are descriptive associations, not evidence of causation.
- Soil features are nearest-profile proxies, not direct borehole measurements.
- Soil profiles are reused across multiple groundwater records; random row-wise train/test splitting can leak information.
- Groundwater target units must be confirmed from Atlas metadata before reporting physical thresholds.
- Missing values were not imputed.
- Soil pH and soil EC must not be described as groundwater pH or groundwater EC.
- The EDA does not establish model suitability or predictive performance.
