IMPORTANT MODELLING RULES
1. Numeric groundwater target columns are retained with their original raw values and quality flags.
2. Missing or invalid target measurements are not imputed.
3. Soil measurements are nearby profile-level proxies, not direct borehole measurements.
4. A soil profile is reused for multiple boreholes. Use grouped/spatial validation so records linked
   to the same soil profile do not leak across train and test sets.
5. Do not use GW_WQ or derived GW_T/GW_S/GW_F/GW_NO3 fields as predictors when predicting
   chemical concentrations. They may encode classifications derived from the target chemistry.
6. Soil pH and soil EC are soil measurements, not groundwater pH or groundwater EC.
7. The sand feature retains the source dataset's definition and should not be treated as a
   conventional sand fraction or used for texture closure until source documentation confirms it.
8. This file is a prepared dataset, not a trained model and not evidence of predictive performance.
